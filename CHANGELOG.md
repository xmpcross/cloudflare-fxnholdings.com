# Changelog

All notable changes to Modal are documented here.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-21

- Fixed grid rail squares cut in half on some sections.
- Fixed product UI screens looking broken on mobile: boards stack their columns, view tabs no longer clip, the dashboard chart fits, and the window fades out at the bottom.
- Updated Astro to 7.4.1 and other dependencies.
- Updated `AGENTS.md` for Astro 7.
- Files check out with LF line endings on every platform.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-15

- Initial commercial release.
- Astro 7 theme for SaaS and product sites with home, pricing, solutions, integrations, customers, about, careers, contact, blog index, topic archive, post, search, RSS, robots, sitemap, and 404 routes.
- Grain gradient background behind the hero, reports, data model cards, solution pages, and closing panel, cropped from one image.
- Tabbed hero product window in a frosted browser frame with dashboard, records, pipeline, and activity screens.
- Product UI built in HTML and CSS: dashboard, records table, pipeline board, record timeline, reports, and AI answer panel.
- Full-width mega menu with a featured card per menu, and a full-screen mobile menu.
- Hairline grid rails and a seven-accent colour system.
- Data model, inbox sync, AI, automations, reports, integrations, customer stories, security, and FAQ sections.
- Integrations marquee with 27 connector icons in their brand colours.
- Pricing page with a billing toggle, four per-seat plans, a seat estimate, and a sticky comparison table.
- Four solution pages from one template.
- Filterable, searchable integrations page.
- Customers, about, careers, and contact pages.
- Markdown and MDX blog with colour-coded topics, co-authors, a table of contents, a call-to-action panel, a share sheet, and search.
- Demo photography for people, blog posts, about, and careers.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, and JSON-LD.
- Self-hosted Onest and JetBrains Mono, Lucide icons, and black-and-white design tokens with colour accents.
