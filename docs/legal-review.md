# Legal pages: review in progress

Reviewed 23 October 2026 for the preview implementation. These changes are not a determination of legal compliance. Production was not changed.

Completed: UK and Australian business disclosures; removal of obsolete chat and browser-storage descriptions; UK data-rights and complaint information; lawful-basis explanation; retention criteria; preservation of UK and Australian mandatory consumer rights; removal of unverified accessibility conformance assertions.

Required factual confirmation before final publication:

- Australian registered legal holder and business structure. Existing pages identify the business name and ABN only.
- Which entity operates the corporate website and controls website/enquiry data. Existing Australian operator retained pending confirmation. Do not infer joint controllers from shared ownership.
- Whether and how enquiry information passes between the two businesses, and whether separate-controller or joint-controller arrangements are needed.
- Email/hosting providers, processor arrangements, storage/access countries, actual retention and deletion practices, and implemented UK transfer safeguards. Policy language does not establish these arrangements.
- Applicability of Australian Privacy Act/APP obligations, UK GDPR territorial scope, any representative or data-protection fee requirements, and the operational complaints procedure.
- Recheck production infrastructure cookies, analytics, live contact form and any assistant before deploying. The revised storage description applies to this preview implementation.
