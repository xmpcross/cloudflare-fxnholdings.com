The updated fxnholdings.com brings our platforms, company information and technology approach into one place. Its purpose is to help visitors understand what FXN Holdings does, find the websites we manage and reach the team with a relevant enquiry.

FXN Holdings brings together businesses in the United Kingdom and Australia under common ownership. Our work spans websites, web applications and online platforms across commerce, publishing, travel, comparison and practical tools. The company website provides an overview of that work and a route to the individual platforms.

This introduction has been updated to reflect the current website, including its navigation and contact options.

## Explore the platforms by what they do

The [platform portfolio](/portfolio/) is the starting point for discovering our websites and tools. It brings the portfolio together with descriptions and links, so visitors can identify a relevant service without first knowing its name.

Different platforms serve different tasks. A comparison site helps someone evaluate options; a publishing site organises information; a practical tool helps complete a specific action. Bringing them into one portfolio makes the range easier to understand without suggesting that every website has the same audience or features.

For questions about a particular service, include its name and page address in your enquiry. That helps distinguish a platform-specific issue from a question about the wider group.

## See how the technology fits together

Our [Technology & AI page](/technology/) explains the relationship between the experience a visitor sees, the content and data behind it, and the systems used to deliver it. It also identifies tools used across our projects, with the combination varying by website.

Shared services support content and comparison platforms. Individual websites retain their own interfaces and releases, while commerce websites use systems suited to their catalogues. This is a practical way to reuse work while allowing each platform to address its audience.

For readers interested in the design choices behind that arrangement, [shared content without identical websites](/insights/shared-content-without-identical-websites/) explores which information can be maintained centrally and which decisions belong to an individual website.

The AI section also states our editorial approach: AI supports research, drafting and content enrichment, while a person reviews AI-assisted content before publication. The accompanying [article on how we use AI](/insights/how-we-use-ai/) explains why that review needs to consider evidence and context as well as wording.

## Find the company context behind the work

The [About page](/about/) introduces FXN Holdings and the relationship between our businesses and platforms. It gives visitors a company-level starting point before they explore a particular website or discuss a project.

That context is especially useful when a platform name is more familiar than the group behind it. A portfolio overview can explain the connection, while the individual platform remains the place to check information specific to its service.

Visitors considering a collaboration can use the company and technology pages together: one explains the business context, and the other explains how we approach building and maintaining websites.

## Read insights with a practical purpose

The [Insights section](/insights/) collects articles on technology, AI, company developments and the questions involved in exploring digital ventures. Each article should help explain a decision or give readers a useful way to assess a problem.

For example, [what we mean by a market gap](/insights/what-we-mean-by-a-market-gap/) starts with the task a person cannot easily complete. It looks at alternatives, evidence and small tests before moving to a platform idea. That is the context behind our phrase “find the gap, build the platform, and launch it”.

The articles complement the main pages by allowing more detail. A portfolio entry can explain what a website does, while an insight can explore the reasoning behind a content process or an architectural choice.

## Get in touch with the right context

The [contact page](/contact/) provides a way to prepare an enquiry in your email application. You can review the message before sending it. Opening the email draft does not send the enquiry automatically.

For a project or partnership, describe the audience, the problem you want to address and any existing website or material we should understand. If you have timing or scope constraints, include those too. You do not need a complete technical specification to explain the opportunity.

For an error or a question about an existing page, send its address and describe the issue. A specific example is easier to investigate than a general report that something seems wrong.

Whether you are finding a tool, learning about our approach or exploring a collaboration, the updated site offers a clearer path from the overview to the detail you need. Start with the portfolio for individual services, the technology page for our approach, or the contact page for a conversation with the team.
